About GKIm Majesty

A Community Built on Faith, Hope, and Love

Our History

GKIm Majesty was founded in 2010 by a small group of believers who felt called to create a welcoming community where people could encounter God's love and grow in their faith. What began as humble gatherings in a living room has blossomed into a vibrant church family dedicated to worshiping God and serving our community.

Over the years, we have witnessed God's faithfulness in bringing together people from diverse backgrounds, united by a common desire to know Christ and make Him known. Our journey has been marked by countless testimonies of transformation, healing, and restored relationships as we've learned to walk together in faith.

Today, GKIm Majesty continues to be a place where seekers find answers, believers find fellowship, and families find a spiritual home. We are grateful for every person who has been part of our story and excited about what God has in store for our future.

Our Vision

Our vision is to be a light in our community—a place where everyone can experience the transforming power of God's love. We dream of a church where people feel welcomed, valued, and equipped to live out their faith in meaningful ways.

We envision GKIm Majesty as a spiritual home where generations come together to worship, learn, and grow. A place where children discover God's love, youth find purpose and direction, adults deepen their faith, and seniors are honored for their wisdom and experience.

We aspire to impact our city and beyond by demonstrating Christ's love through acts of compassion, service, and genuine relationships. Our vision extends beyond our church walls as we seek to be salt and light, making a positive difference in the lives of those around us.

Our Mission

Our mission is to glorify God by making disciples of Jesus Christ who love God, love people, and serve the world. We are committed to creating an environment where people can encounter God through authentic worship, biblical teaching, and genuine community.

We strive to help every member grow in their relationship with Christ through regular worship services, Bible studies, prayer gatherings, and fellowship activities. We believe that spiritual growth happens best in the context of loving relationships where people can share their joys, struggles, and journey of faith.

Beyond our walls, we are dedicated to serving our community with compassion and excellence. Whether through outreach programs, helping those in need, or simply being good neighbors, we seek to demonstrate God's love in practical ways. Our mission is not just what we do on Sundays, but how we live every day of the week.

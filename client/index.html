ABOUT US

Grace Chapel Church is a vibrant and welcoming community dedicated to spreading God's love and serving our neighbors. Since our founding, we've been committed to creating a space where everyone can grow in their faith journey.

We believe in the power of community, the importance of worship, and the transformative nature of God's grace. Our mission is to inspire and equip believers to live out their faith in meaningful ways.

Whether you're new to faith or have been walking with God for years, you'll find a home here with us. Join us for worship services, community events, and opportunities to serve.
